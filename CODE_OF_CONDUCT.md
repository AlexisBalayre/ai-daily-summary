# Code of Conduct

Be kind, be constructive, assume good faith. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are
not tolerated in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that do not respect this, and
may block repeat offenders.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer listed in `pyproject.toml`. Reports are handled
privately.

This document is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
